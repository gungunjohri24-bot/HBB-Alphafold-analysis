# HBB Mutation 

## Selected mutation

- Gene: HBB
- Protein: Hemoglobin subunit beta
- UniProt accession: P68871
- Mutation: p.Glu7Val (E7V)
- Alternative numbering: p.Glu6Val (E6V)
- DNA change: c.20A>T
- dbSNP: rs334
- Common name: HbS

## Why I selected it

I selected p.Glu7Val for the normal-vs-mutant comparison because it is a well-documented HBB missense variant with established clinical significance.

## What I will compare

In the next stage, I will compare the normal HBB sequence with the mutant sequence to examine:

1. The amino-acid sequence difference.
2. The location of the mutation in the predicted structure.
3. pLDDT around the mutation.
4. Differences between the predicted normal and mutant structures.
5. Changes in predicted confidence, if any.

## Important note

The mutation is commonly called E6V because mature hemoglobin numbering excludes the initial methionine. In the 147-amino-acid P68871 reference sequence used in this project, the corresponding position is residue 7 (Glu → Val).

These observations will not be treated as direct evidence of disease mechanism or protein stability.
